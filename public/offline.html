KROMA is offline

There is no connection right now, so nothing can be measured. Every colour in this app comes from a measurement made on a photo you take — none of it is stored in advance, and none of it can be guessed.

Reconnect and reload, and everything you had measured is still on this device.

Your wardrobe, your profile and your colour portrait live in this browser, not on a server. Being offline does not lose them.
